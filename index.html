Cornerways Worth Matravers
The main event

Walks & Runs

A genuinely spectacular stretch of the Jurassic Coast with walks that start from the village — most of it good for running as well as walking, with route options from a gentle half hour to a full day out.

🧭 Further afield, still an easy drive

Durlston Country Park and Anvil Point (coastal path running with a proper cliff-top lighthouse), Old Harry Rocks near Studland, and the climb up to Corfe Castle for wide views over the whole Isle of Purbeck.

Nearby

Places to Eat

In Worth itself, the Square and Compass and tea, coffee and cake from the little shop by the pond, or a few miles to other pubs in nearby villages and restaurants in Swanage.

Beyond the path

Places to Visit

The rest of the Isle of Purbeck, all within easy reach.

🔥 Heating & hot water
  • Storage heaters manual
  • Each heater has a display and a dial, just like a normal thermostat — rotate the dial to set your desired room temperature (21°C is a comfortable default) and it takes care of overnight charging and heat release automatically.
  • If a room needs to be warmer quickly, press Menu then select Boost on the heater for a temporary top-up.
  • The showers are electric and produce hot water on demand — no need to plan ahead for those.
  • For hot water for the bath and sinks, this comes from a hot water tank — use the boost button in the cupboard under the stairs if you need extra.
