🔥 Heating

  1. Heating is by storage heaters — each one quietly charges up overnight using cheaper off-peak electricity, then releases that heat through the day.
  2. Each heater has an input dial (how much heat it charges overnight) and an output/boost dial or damper (how much heat it releases now). Turn output up if a room is cold.
  3. They can feel cool in the morning and warm up through the day — that's normal, not a fault.
  4. Full manufacturer's manual is provided separately if you'd like more detail.

🚿 Hot Water

  1. Hot water for the bath and sinks is heated by an immersion-style system.
  2. If you need extra hot water, press the boost button in the cupboard under the stairs.
  3. Boosting takes a little while to heat up — turn it on ahead of a bath if you can.
